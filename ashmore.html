Ashmore Engineering & Structural Specs

N25/N32 High-Density Concrete Mix

Certified 25 MPa or 32 MPa concrete mixes engineered for high compressive strength, surface abrasion resistance, and lasting durability under Queensland weather conditions.

Elevated SL72/SL82 Steel Mesh

High-tensile steel mesh elevated on 50mm plastic bar chairs ensures 30mm–40mm concrete top cover to absorb tensile load and eliminate shrinkage cracking.

75mm–100mm Compacted Roadbase Foundation

Mechanically compacted DGB20 roadbase sub-base over Ashmore's alluvial subgrade guarantees uniform load distribution and prevents slab settling.

Diamond Saw-Cut Expansion Joints

Precision diamond saw-cut control joints cut within 12–24 hours every 3 meters relieve internal thermal stress and ensure long-term crack-free aesthetics.

Professional Execution

Our Concreting Process: Excavation to Sealing

01

Excavation & Base Prep

We excavate to depth, remove old cracked concrete or soft topsoil, mechanically compact the subgrade, and install a 75mm–100mm compacted roadbase sub-base.

02

Formwork & Steel Laying

We set up straight timber formwork, lay heavy plastic vapor moisture barrier, and position SL72/SL82 reinforcement steel mesh on plastic support chairs.

03

Concrete Pouring & Finishing

We pour certified N25/N32 concrete mix, screed to precise levels with cross-fall drainage, and apply the final finish—whether exposed aggregate wash, broom finish, or stenciling.

04

Cutting & Sealing

We cut expansion control joints within 24 hours to manage thermal stress and apply two coats of UV-stable solvent acrylic concrete sealer for lasting stain protection.

Ashmore FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions — Ashmore Concreting

Q: Can old cracked 1980s concrete driveways in Ashmore be spray resurfaced with Covercrete?

A: Yes! If the existing concrete slab is structurally sound, we diamond-grind the surface, repair cracks with high-strength polymer resin, and spray-apply polymer-modified Covercrete with custom slate/stone patterns and anti-slip silica sand sealer.

Q: How much does a new concrete driveway cost in Ashmore (4214)?

A: Pricing in Ashmore depends on square meterage, whether old slab demolition is required, chosen finish (exposed aggregate, plain, or Covercrete resurfacing), and site access. Call us on 0411 914 157 or submit our online form for a free on-site measure and quote.

Q: How long does a full driveway replacement take in Ashmore?

A: A standard 50m²–80m² driveway replacement in Ashmore takes approximately 2 to 3 days: Day 1 for demolition, excavation, and roadbase compaction; Day 2 for formwork, steel mesh, and concrete pouring; Day 3 for pressure washing and UV acrylic sealing.

Q: Do you pour non-slip exposed aggregate around swimming pools in Ashmore?

A: Yes. Exposed aggregate is one of the most popular finishes for Ashmore pool surrounds because the natural rounded pebbles provide exceptional wet traction, resist chlorine and salt water, and stay cooler underfoot during summer.

Q: Do I need a Gold Coast Council VXO permit for a driveway crossover in Ashmore?

A: Yes. Any driveway crossing extending across public council land to the street kerb line requires a Vehicular Crossing (VXO) permit from the City of Gold Coast. We handle site drawings, formwork setups, and pre-pour inspections for you.
