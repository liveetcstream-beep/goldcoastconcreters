Our Concreting Process: Excavation to Sealing

01

Excavation & Base Prep

We excavate to depth, remove organic soil, mechanically compact the sandy subgrade, and lay a compacted roadbase sub-base for maximum load distribution.

02

Formwork & Steel Laying

We set up precision timber formwork, lay heavy-duty plastic vapor barrier, and position SL82 reinforcement steel mesh on non-corrosive plastic support chairs.

03

Concrete Pouring & Finishing

We pour marine-grade N32/N40 concrete mix, screed to exact levels, and apply the final finish—whether non-slip exposed aggregate wash, broom finish, or stenciling.

04

Cutting & Sealing

We cut expansion control joints within 24 hours and apply two coats of premium UV & salt-resistant acrylic concrete sealer for maximum longevity.

Coastal Marine Environment & Sand Subgrade Dynamics

Properties in Burleigh Heads sit on sandy marine soils subject to moisture fluctuations and salt-laden ocean winds. Unreinforced or poorly compacted concrete poured on beach sand risks cracking or edge breakdown. We stabilize Burleigh Heads concrete by excavating organic matter, compacting a heavy 50mm roadbase sub-base, supporting SL82 steel mesh on non-corrosive plastic chairs, and pouring N32/N40 marine-grade concrete with deep control joints cut per AS 2870 (Residential Slabs & Footings).

Burleigh Heads FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions — Burleigh Heads Concreting

Q: How do you protect concrete from salt air corrosion in Burleigh Heads?

A: Coastal salt air accelerates rebar rust if moisture penetrates the slab. In Burleigh Heads, we use marine-grade N32/N40 concrete, elevate SL82 steel mesh on non-corrosive plastic chairs, ensure a minimum 50mm steel cover, and seal all exposed surfaces with UV-stable acrylic sealers.

Q: How much does a concrete driveway or pool deck cost in Burleigh Heads (4220)?

A: Pricing in Burleigh Heads depends on square meterage, chosen finish (exposed aggregate, plain, or Covercrete resurfacing), site access, and excavation requirements. Call us on 0411 914 157 or submit our online form for a free on-site measure and quote.

Q: How do you stabilize concrete poured on coastal sand subgrades?

A: Sandy subgrade requires thorough mechanical compaction using vibratory plate compactors and the installation of a 50mm compacted roadbase sub-base. This prevents sand displacement and slab settling under vehicle loads.

Q: Can existing weathered concrete driveways in Burleigh Heads be resurfaced with Covercrete?

A: Yes! If your existing concrete slab is structurally sound, we diamond-grind the surface to remove salt residue, repair hairline cracks with polymer resin, and spray-apply Covercrete with anti-slip silica sand sealer.

Q: Do I need a Gold Coast Council VXO permit for a driveway crossover in Burleigh Heads?

A: Yes. Any driveway crossing extending across public council land to the street kerb line requires a Vehicular Crossing (VXO) permit from the City of Gold Coast. We handle site drawings, formwork setups, and pre-pour inspections for you.
