Professional Execution

Our Concreting Process: Excavation to Sealing

01

Excavation & Base Prep

We excavate to required depth, remove unstable silt, mechanically compact the subgrade with heavy vibratory rollers, and install a 100mm compacted roadbase foundation.

02

Formwork & Steel Laying

We set up straight timber formwork, install heavy plastic vapor barriers, and position SL82 reinforcement steel mesh on plastic support chairs.

03

Concrete Pouring & Finishing

We pour certified N32/N40 concrete mix, screed to precise levels with cross-fall drainage, and apply the final finish—commercial broom, exposed aggregate wash, or stenciling.

04

Cutting & Sealing

We cut expansion control joints within 24 hours to manage thermal stress and apply two coats of UV-stable solvent acrylic concrete sealer for lasting protection.

Bundall FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions — Bundall Concreting

Q: What concrete specifications are required for commercial driveways in Bundall?

A: Commercial driveways and loading aprons along Upton Street and Ashmore Road require a minimum 150mm slab thickness, N32 (32 MPa) concrete mix, SL82 steel mesh on 65mm chairs, 100mm compacted roadbase, and heavy broom finish for vehicle traction.

Q: How much does a concrete driveway or commercial slab cost in Bundall (4217)?

A: Pricing in Bundall depends on project size, slab thickness (residential 100mm vs commercial 150mm), excavation needs, chosen finish (exposed aggregate, plain, or Covercrete resurfacing), and steel mesh grade. Call us on 0411 914 157 for a free on-site measure and quote.

Q: How do you protect concrete pool surrounds from canal salt water in Sorrento?

A: For Sorrento canal properties in Bundall, we use marine-grade N32/N40 concrete, support steel mesh on non-corrosive plastic bar chairs to ensure 40mm top cover, and apply two coats of premium UV-stable salt-resistant acrylic sealer on all exposed surfaces.

Q: Can old commercial showroom floors or driveways in Bundall be resurfaced with Covercrete?

A: Yes! If the existing concrete base is structurally sound, we diamond-grind the surface, fill cracks with high-strength polymer resin, and spray-apply Covercrete with heavy-duty anti-slip silica sand sealer and custom patterns.

Q: Do I need a Gold Coast Council VXO permit for a driveway crossover in Bundall?

A: Yes. Any commercial or residential driveway crossing extending across public council road reserve requires a Vehicular Crossing (VXO) permit from the City of Gold Coast. We handle site drawings, formwork setups, and pre-pour inspections for you.
