Our History

Built on Local Queensland Knowledge

Gold Coast Concreters QLD began over 15 years ago with a simple mission: to provide South East Queensland homeowners and businesses with structural, long-lasting concrete solutions that resist subtropical climate challenges.

Having poured thousands of square metres of concrete across the Gold Coast corridor, we understand the local soil conditions intimately — from the reactive black clay soils (Class H1/H2) in Pimpama (4209) and Ormeau (4208) to coastal salt spray environments near Southport (4215) and Hope Island (4212).

We take pride in doing every job right the first time: excavating proper sub-base depths, compacting CBR 15 roadbase, elevating steel mesh on bar chairs, and saw-cutting expansion control joints at exact intervals.

Our Philosophy

4 Principles That Guide Every Concreting Pour

📐

Strict Engineering Compliance

We strictly adhere to Australian Standard AS 3727.1-2016 for residential pavements and AS 2870 for house foundation slabs. No cutting corners on steel mesh or slab depth.

📋

Transparent Itemised Quotes

No surprise fees for pump hire, earthworks excavation, or waste cartage. We outline every material specification clearly in our written estimates.

🏛️

Council Compliance & VXO

We manage the entire City of Gold Coast Vehicular Crossing (VXO) approval process, kerb layback cuts, pre-pour council checks, and Form 16 sign-offs.

🛡️

6.5-Year Structural Warranty

All residential and commercial concrete installations are backed by full QBCC statutory warranties and $10M public liability insurance protection.
