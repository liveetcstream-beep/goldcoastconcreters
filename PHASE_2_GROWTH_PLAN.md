## Track 3: Conversion Optimization (Weeks 2–6)

More leads from the same traffic without building any new content.

### 3.1 Call Tracking Setup

**Problem:** When your dad misses calls, you lose the lead permanently.

**Solution:** Replace the static phone number `0411 914 157` on the website with a **tracked virtual number** that:
- Rings your dad's phone AND your phone in Pakistan simultaneously.
- Records all calls.
- Sends an SMS transcript immediately after each call.
- Sends a missed-call text to the customer automatically.

**Recommended Tool:** [CallRail](https://www.callrail.com) or [Twilio](https://www.twilio.com)
- Cost: AUD $30–$60/month
- Benefit: You never miss a lead again.

---

Set up an auto-SMS that fires whenever the phone rings and is not answered within 20 seconds:

This alone recovers 60%+ of missed calls by getting the customer to text back their job details.
