Our Concreting Process: Excavation to Sealing

01

Excavation & Base Prep

We excavate to depth, remove topsoil, mechanically compact the subgrade, and lay a compacted roadbase foundation for proper load distribution.

02

Formwork & Steel Laying

We set up straight timber formwork, lay plastic moisture underlay if required, and position SL82 reinforcement mesh on plastic support chairs.

03

Concrete Pouring & Finishing

We pour N32/N25 concrete mix, screed to level, and apply the final finish—whether heavy broom (for crossovers), exposed aggregate wash, or stenciling.

04

Cutting & Sealing

We cut control joints to manage expansion stress and apply two coats of UV-stable solvent acrylic concrete sealer to protect the slab from dirt and stains.

Why Pacific Pines Homeowners Choose Gold Coast Concreters QLD

Pacific Pines (4211) is a flourishing master-planned family community nestled against the Gold Coast hinterland foothills, anchored by Central Park, Pitcairn Way, and Pacific Pines Town Centre. Due to the suburb's elevated, undulating topography, many residential blocks feature steep driveway inclines and severe rainwater runoff. As QBCC-licensed concrete specialists active weekly across Pacific Pines, we engineer non-slip exposed aggregate driveways, reinforced hillside steps, and retaining wall footings built to manage sloping ground dynamics.

Hillside Soil Dynamics & Sloped Terrain in Pacific Pines

Pacific Pines sits on rocky-shale subsoils interspersed with clay. Driveways poured on sloped hillside ground risk washing out or cracking under vehicle weight if sub-base compaction is skipped. We stabilize Pacific Pines hillside driveways by excavating to rock subgrade, compacting a 50mm roadbase sub-base, placing SL82 steel reinforcement mesh on bar chairs, and cutting expansion joints every 3 metres per AS 2870 (Residential Slabs & Footings).

Pacific Pines Project Scope & Case Study

We recently designed a project scope for a 48m² exposed aggregate driveway replacement on a sloped block near Central Park off Pitcairn Way. The job involved excavating an old 100mm cracked plain slab, laying 50mm roadbase, installing SL82 steel mesh on chairs, pouring slip-resistant N32 concrete with Gold Coast pebbles, and applying a heavy-duty UV acrylic sealer — finished in 3 days.

Pacific Pines FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions — Pacific Pines Concreting

Q: How do you handle steep hillside driveways off Pitcairn Way and Central Park?

A: Steep inclines in Pacific Pines require reduced-slump N32 concrete mixes, heavy SL82 steel mesh elevated on 50mm chairs, deep footings anchored into clay-shale subgrade, and high-traction exposed aggregate or heavy broom finishes to prevent vehicle tire slippage in wet weather.

Q: How much does a concrete driveway cost in Pacific Pines (4211)?

A: Driveway pricing in Pacific Pines depends on slope steepness, excavation requirements, chosen finish (exposed aggregate, plain, or Covercrete resurfacing), and square meterage. Call us on 0411 914 157 or submit our online form for a free on-site measure and quote.

Q: How do you prevent rainwater runoff damage on sloped Pacific Pines blocks?

A: We engineer custom swale drainage channels, install heavy-duty trench drains at the base of steep driveways, and pour concrete with a 1:100 cross-fall to divert tropical storm water safely into council stormwater pits.

Q: Can old discolored Pacific Pines driveways be spray resurfaced with Covercrete?

A: Yes! If your existing concrete slab is structurally sound, we diamond-grind the surface, repair hairline cracks with polymer resin, and spray-apply Covercrete with a custom slate pattern and anti-slip silica sand sealer.

Q: Do I need a Gold Coast Council VXO permit for a driveway in Pacific Pines?

A: Yes. Any driveway crossing extending across public council land to the street kerb line requires a Vehicular Crossing (VXO) permit from the City of Gold Coast. We handle site drawings, formwork setups, and pre-pour inspections for you.
