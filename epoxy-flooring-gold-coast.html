Surface Preparation

2. Mechanical Diamond Grinding: The Secret to Zero Peeling

The number one cause of epoxy flooring failure on the Gold Coast is improper surface preparation. Store-bought acid etching kits fail to penetrate hard trowelled concrete, leaving a smooth surface that releases when vehicle tires park after a long drive along the M1 motorway.

  • Heavy Dustless Diamond Grinding: We operate planetary diamond head grinders connected to HEPA dust extractors. This grinds away weak surface laitance, opening concrete capillaries to achieve an International Concrete Repair Institute (ICRI) CSP 2 to CSP 3 profile.
  • Epoxy Mortar Crack Repair: Before applying primers, all hairline cracks, expansion joints, and pitted gouges are filled with two-component structural epoxy filler and ground flush.
  • Moisture Vapor Barrier (MVB): Gold Coast ground slabs in low-lying areas like Hope Island (4212) or Coomera (4209) can suffer from hydrostatic groundwater pressure. We test slab moisture levels and apply specialized MVB primers to block moisture vapor from blistering the epoxy.
Layered Protection

3. Anatomy of a 100% Solid Garage Flake Floor

Our signature residential garage flooring consists of 3 heavy-duty industrial layers:

Layer 1: 100% Solids Epoxy Primer Base Coat

A high-penetration 2-part epoxy resin colored to match your selected flake blend. It bonds deep into the open concrete pores, forming a permanent structural anchor.

Layer 2: Full Broadcast Vinyl Decorative Chips

We broadcast premium UV-stable vinyl flakes until the floor is 100% saturated ("broadcast to rejection"). This builds a 1.5mm thick impact-cushioning matrix that hides minor concrete imperfections and adds rich color texture (popular blends include Domino, Nightfall, Outback, and Basalt).

Layer 3: Polyaspartic Clear Topcoat

Unlike cheap polyurethane or epoxy clear coats that turn yellow under Gold Coast sun exposure, our polyaspartic topcoat is 100% UV-stable, ultra-clear, highly scratch-resistant, and cures 4 times faster than standard epoxy.

Common Questions

FAQs — Epoxy Flooring Gold Coast

How long does a garage epoxy flake floor last on the Gold Coast?

When installed over a diamond-ground concrete base with a 100% solids epoxy resin binder and UV-stable polyaspartic topcoat, residential garage flake epoxy floors last 15 to 20+ years without peeling, cracking, or hot-tire pickup.

What causes DIY hardware store epoxy paints to peel up under car tires?

Single-pack DIY epoxy paints stick only to surface dust because home acid-washing fails to open concrete pores. When hot tires park on the slab, hot-tire pickup pulls the thin paint right off. Professional installers use dustless diamond grinding (CSP 2 to 3 profile) and 100% solid commercial epoxy resins that fuse permanently into the concrete matrix.

Is epoxy flooring slip-resistant when wet?

Decorative vinyl flake epoxy systems naturally feature a textured surface that provides excellent slip resistance. For ramps, commercial kitchens, or wet work bays, we incorporate aluminum oxide or glass micro-bead anti-slip media into the polyaspartic topcoat to achieve an AS 4586 P4 or P5 safety rating.

How long does it take to install a double garage epoxy floor?

A standard 2-car garage flake floor installation takes 1 to 2 days. Day 1 involves diamond grinding, joint filling, base coat resin application, and full vinyl flake broadcast. Day 2 includes scraping excess flake, vacuuming, and applying the clear polyaspartic topcoat. Foot traffic is allowed 24 hours later, and vehicle parking after 48 to 72 hours.

Can epoxy flooring be installed over old or cracked concrete?

Yes. Prior to applying epoxy base coats, we fill all cracks, spalls, and pitted concrete joints with heavy-duty polymer epoxy mortars and grind the slab flat. If hydrostatic ground moisture is present, we apply a specialized moisture vapor barrier (MVB) primer first.
