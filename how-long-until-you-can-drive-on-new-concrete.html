⚡ Quick Summary: Curing Timeline for Gold Coast Concrete

  • 24 to 48 Hours: You can safely walk on the concrete (light pedestrian foot traffic and domestic pets). Keep garden tools, wheelbarrows, and heavy bins off the slab.
  • 7 Days (The Light Car Threshold): Concrete reaches approximately 70% of its ultimate design compressive strength. Standard passenger cars (sedans, hatchbacks under 1.8 tonnes) can now safely drive and park on the driveway.
  • 14 Days: Safe for larger SUVs, trade utes (under 3 tonnes), and double-axle box trailers.
  • 28 Days (Full Structural Maturity): The concrete has completed its primary hydration process, achieving 100% of its rated MPa strength (e.g. 25–32 MPa). You can now park heavy 4WDs, boat trailers, heavy caravans, horse floats, and delivery trucks.
  • Queensland Heat Factor: In hot Gold Coast summer conditions, concrete sets faster on the surface but loses internal moisture rapidly. We apply curing compounds or light hose misting during the first 3 days to prevent plastic shrinkage cracking.
Curing Stages

The Concrete Hydration & Strength Timeline

24h

Initial Set — Foot Traffic Only

The chemical hydration reaction binds water and cement molecules. The surface is firm to the touch. You may walk across the driveway to check your letterbox, but keep bicycle tires, skateboards, and sharp shoes off.

7d

70% Compressive Strength — Light Vehicles

Standard sedans and hatchbacks can now drive on the slab. Pro Tip: Avoid turning your steering wheel while stationary (dry steering), as sticky tire rubber can leave black scuff marks on freshly sealed concrete.

14d

85% Compressive Strength — SUVs & Utes

Medium commercial trade vehicles, 4WD wagons, and landscaping equipment can access the driveway without risk of surface indentation or edge cracking.

28d

100% Ultimate Strength — Heavy Loads & Boats

The slab has reached full engineered hardness (N25 / N32 MPa). Heavy triple-axle caravans, offshore fishing boats on trailers, skip bin trucks, and moving vans can safely load without damaging the slab.

Trade Estimator Note — Gold Coast Concreting Network (QBCC Licensed Partners)

"The number one cause of early driveway cracking on the Gold Coast is driving a heavy 4WD or fully-laden tradie trailer onto the slab on day 3 or 4 because 'it looked hard'. At day 4, concrete has only reached about 50% strength. The weight creates micro-fissures around the SL72/SL82 steel mesh that won't show immediately, but will expand into visible cracks over the next 12 months. Be patient: give it 7 full days for cars and 28 days for heavy rigs."

— Gold Coast Concreters Trade Estimators | QBCC Licensed Partner Network
