Our Concreting Process: Excavation to Sealing

01

Excavation & Base Prep

We excavate acreage tracks to depth, clear vegetation, compact subgrade with plate compactors, and lay a compacted roadbase or crusher dust foundation.

02

Formwork & Steel Laying

We set up straight timber formwork along property contours, lay plastic moisture underlay if required, and position SL82 reinforcement mesh on plastic support chairs.

03

Concrete Pouring & Finishing

We pour N32/N25 concrete mix, screed to level, and apply the final finish—whether heavy broom, exposed aggregate wash, or stenciling.

04

Cutting & Sealing

We cut control joints to manage expansion stress and apply two coats of premium UV-stable solvent acrylic concrete sealer to protect the slab from dirt and stains.

Valley Creek & Soil Dynamics in Willow Vale

Valley soils near Hotham Creek contain rich alluvial clay pockets that retain moisture during summer wet seasons. Unreinforced slabs on uncompacted valley soil shift and sink over time. We stabilize Willow Vale rural ground by excavating subgrade, compacting a 50mm-100mm roadbase sub-base, placing SL82 heavy steel mesh elevated on bar chairs, and deepening perimeter edge beams in compliance with AS 2870 (Residential Slabs & Footings).

Willow Vale Acreage Project Scope & Case Study

We recently completed a 140m² sloping acreage driveway and 9x7m machinery barn slab project off Ruffles Road in Willow Vale. The job required excavating 200mm of heavy clay subsoil near Hotham Creek catchment, compacting a 100mm roadbase sub-base, placing SL82 steel mesh on 50mm bar chairs, utilizing a line pump for steep site access, pouring N32 grade concrete with natural Gold Coast pebbles, and installing swale drainage channels — finished in 4 days.

Q: Can you pour long sloping acreage driveways in Willow Vale?

A: Yes. We specialize in rural acreage concreting for sloping terrain in Willow Vale, utilizing concrete line pumps, heavy SL82 steel mesh reinforcement, and saw-cut expansion joints for long access tracks.

Q: Do I need a Gold Coast City Council permit for a driveway in Willow Vale?

A: You only need a GCCC Vehicular Crossing (VXO) permit for the section of your driveway extending from your property boundary to the kerb line over council land. Private internal acreage driveways do not require permits.
