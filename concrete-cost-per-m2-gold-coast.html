How to Spot Fake "Cheap" Concreting Quotes ($40-$50/m²)

"In my 15 years estimating concrete projects across the Gold Coast, I frequently see homeowners get caught out by dodgy quotes offering $45 or $50 per square meter. Here is how dodgy operators pull off that price: they pour 75mm thin concrete instead of the standard 100mm, they lay steel mesh directly on dirt without plastic bar chairs, they use weak 20MPA high-water mix, and they leave out saw-cut expansion joints."

"Within 12 months, the thin slab snaps under the weight of a 4WD or SUV, and the contractor is nowhere to be found. Always demand a written QBCC quote stating 100mm depth, N25/N32 concrete grade, SL72 mesh on chairs, 100mm roadbase, and saw cuts."

Cost Drivers

4 Hidden Factors That Influence Your Final Concreting Bill

1. Concrete Line & Boom Pump Hire

If access to your backyard or driveway is tight (common in Coomera (4209) and Helensvale (4212)), a concrete pump is required. Pump hire adds a flat fee of $600 to $900 per pour.

2. Clay Excavation & Soil Disposal

Excavating heavy black clay in Pimpama (4209) or Ormeau (4208) requires bobcat excavation and tip truck disposal fees ($40-$60 per m³ dumped at local QLD waste facilities).

3. GCCC Council Crossover Inspection

For vehicular crossovers (VXO), Gold Coast City Council requires application fees and Form 16 QBCC engineering sign-offs, adding roughly $300-$500 to the crossover portion.

4. Sloped Site Formwork & Drainage

Steep sloped driveways in Pacific Pines (4211) require extra timber boxing formwork, deep edge thickenings, and trench drains to capture stormwater runoff.

Pricing FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Gold Coast Concrete Costs

How much does exposed aggregate concrete cost per m2 on the Gold Coast?

Exposed aggregate concrete costs between $95 and $145 per square meter for standard 100mm depth installation with SL72 mesh. Premium stone aggregates (such as dark quartz or ocean pebble mixes) or sloped site pours requiring a concrete pump range from $130 to $160 per m².

How much does a 50m² concrete driveway cost in Gold Coast QLD?

A standard double-car driveway (~50m²) in plain broom-finish concrete costs approximately $3,500 to $4,500. An exposed aggregate 50m² driveway ranges from $5,000 to $7,200 including site excavation, 100mm roadbase sub-grade, steel mesh, saw-cut expansion joints, and clear acrylic sealer.

Is covercrete resurfacing cheaper than replacing a concrete driveway?

Yes! Covercrete spray-on driveway resurfacing costs between $45 and $75 per m², whereas full demolition, removal, soil disposal, and repouring a new exposed aggregate slab costs between $120 and $180 per m² total. Covercrete saves up to 50% to 60% of total replacement cost.

What hidden extra costs should I watch out for in cheap concreting quotes?

Beware of quotes under $50/m². Cheap contractors often omit essential items: earthmoving & soil disposal ($400-$800 extra), concrete pump hire ($600-$900 flat fee), SL72 steel mesh, plastic bar chairs, saw-cut expansion joints, or UV sealer. Always ensure your QBCC quote explicitly itemises all sub-base preparation.
