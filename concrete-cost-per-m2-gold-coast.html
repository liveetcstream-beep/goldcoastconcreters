Cost Drivers

4 Key Factors That Determine Your Final Concreting Quote

1. Concrete Line & Pump Access

If concrete agitator trucks cannot reverse directly up to your pour area, a concrete pump line is required to pump wet concrete to the backyard.

2. Soil Type & Earthworks Excavation

Excavating heavy black clay in Pimpama (4209) or Ormeau (4208) requires bobcat machinery and waste disposal truck cartage.

3. GCCC Council Crossover Approvals

For driveway crossovers (VXO) crossing public verges, Gold Coast City Council requires application reviews and Form 16 QBCC engineering sign-offs.

4. Sloped Site Formwork & Drainage

Steep sloped blocks in Pacific Pines (4211) require extra timber boxing formwork, edge thickenings, and trench drains for stormwater management.

Pricing FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Concreting Pricing

How are concrete prices calculated on the Gold Coast?

Concreting quotes are calculated based on total area (sqm), earthworks excavation depth, concrete mix strength (N25/N32), steel mesh grade (SL72/SL82), decorative finish type, and site access. Because every site is different, an on-site measure is required for an accurate written quote.

Is covercrete resurfacing cheaper than replacing an existing driveway?

Yes! Covercrete spray-on resurfacing avoids heavy demolition, jackhammering, bobcat excavation, and concrete waste disposal fees. It typically saves up to 50% to 60% compared to the cost of a full driveway replacement.

What factors influence the final cost of a concrete driveway?

Key cost factors include site slope, soil reactivity (clay vs sand), steel mesh elevation chairs, concrete pump requirements for tight access, saw-cut expansion joint layout, and UV sealer application.
