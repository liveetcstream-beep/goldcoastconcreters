Our Concreting Process: Excavation to Sealing

01

Excavation & Base Preparation

We excavate the site to precise depth, remove vegetation and old concrete, mechanically compact the subgrade, and lay a dense compacted roadbase or crusher dust base layer.

02

Formwork & Steel Placement

We set up rigid timber formwork to define exact slab lines, lay 200-micron moisture membrane underlay, and support SL82 steel mesh on bar chairs for optimal reinforcement position.

03

Concrete Pouring & Precision Finishing

We pour high-grade N32/N25 premix concrete, screed to level grade, and apply the chosen finish—whether heavy broom non-slip finish, exposed aggregate pebble wash, or Covercrete spray.

04

Control Joint Cutting & Dual Sealing

We cut stress control joints within 24 hours to prevent random cracking, wash the cured surface, and apply two coats of premium UV-stable solvent acrylic sealer for long-lasting protection.

Frequently Asked Questions - Oxenford Concreting

Q: How much does a concrete driveway cost in Oxenford?

A: Driveway concreting costs depend on total square meterage, chosen finish (plain broom, exposed aggregate, or Covercrete resurfacing), ground slope, and excavation requirements. Because every site in Oxenford is unique, we do not provide generic online estimates. Call us on 0411 914 157 or submit our form for a free, exact, no-obligation on-site quote.

Q: Can old discolored driveways in Studio Village be resurfaced?

A: Yes! As long as the existing concrete slab is structurally sound, we grind away old dirt and oil stains, patch minor cracks, and apply polymer-modified Covercrete spray-on resurfacing with custom border patterns for a fraction of full replacement cost.

Q: Do I need a Gold Coast City Council permit for a driveway crossover in Oxenford?

A: You need a Gold Coast City Council Vehicular Crossing (VXO) permit for the crossover portion extending across council land from your property boundary to the road kerb. Our licensed team manages all drawings, council applications, and pre-pour inspections for you.

Q: How do you handle steep sloping driveways in Oxenford's elevated areas?

A: For steep slopes near the Tamborine foothills, we pour N32 strength concrete with heavily textured exposed aggregate or broom finishes for superior tire grip. We also integrate trench drains and cut extra control joints to manage runoff and slab stress.

Q: What concrete thickness is required for heavy caravans or boats in Oxenford?

A: Standard car driveways use 100mm thick concrete. For heavy caravans, boats, or work trucks, we recommend a 125mm-150mm thick pour using N32 grade concrete with heavy SL82 steel mesh reinforcement to prevent cracking under high axle loads.

Q: What neighboring Gold Coast suburbs do you service around Oxenford?

A: Alongside Oxenford (4210), we regularly pour concrete in Studio Village, Helensvale (4212), Pacific Pines (4211), Coomera (4209), Hope Island (4212), and Ormeau (4208).
