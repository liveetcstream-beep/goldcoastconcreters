Oxenford Engineering & Pour Specifications

N25 & N32 Concrete Strength

We pour N25 (25MPa) grade concrete for garden paths and shed slabs. For vehicle driveways and heavy traffic in Oxenford, we use N32 (32MPa) concrete for high load capacity.

SL82 Steel Mesh Reinforcement

All Oxenford slabs feature heavy SL82 steel mesh laid on plastic bar chairs to ensure maximum structural reinforcement in the center of the concrete pour.

Proper Subgrade Compaction

We prevent shifting and cracking by compacting the subgrade and laying a 50mm compacted roadbase layer before installing steel mesh and pouring.

Expansion Control Joints

We cut control joints at precise intervals to prevent cracking and place flexible expansion joints where new concrete meets existing house slabs or walls.

Professional Execution

Our Concreting Process: Excavation to Sealing

01

Excavation & Base Prep

We excavate to depth, remove grass and roots, compact the subgrade using a mechanical plate compactor, and lay a compacted roadbase or crusher dust foundation.

02

Formwork & Steel Laying

We set up straight timber formwork to define slab edges, lay plastic moisture underlay if required, and position SL82 reinforcement mesh on plastic support chairs.

03

Concrete Pouring & Finishing

We pour N32/N25 concrete mix, screed to level, and apply the final finish—whether heavy broom (for crossovers), exposed aggregate wash, or stenciling.

04

Cutting & Sealing

We cut control joints to manage expansion stress and apply two coats of UV-stable solvent acrylic concrete sealer to protect the slab from dirt and stains.
