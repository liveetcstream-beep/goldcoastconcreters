Critical Safety

5 Costly Mistakes to Avoid When Concreting Around Swimming Pools

1. High-Gloss Sealers (Slippery Ice Hazard)

Applying standard high-gloss acrylic driveway sealer near a pool creates a smooth glass surface that turns dangerously slippery when wet. We apply semi-penetrating silane sealers infused with Shark Grip non-slip polypropylene beads for AS 4586 P4 compliance.

2. Dark Aggregates Burning Barefoot Feet

Dark charcoal or black basalt aggregate absorbs extreme heat under Gold Coast summer sun (35°C+), reaching surface temperatures over 65°C. We specify light quartz, cream, and coastal sandstone mixes that reflect solar heat and stay cool underfoot.

3. Missing Coping Bond Beam Isolation Joints

A swimming pool shell vibrates and expands independently from the surround slab. Pouring concrete tight against the pool coping without a 10mm Ableflex isolation joint will cause the surround slab to shear off coping tiles during seasonal soil movement.

4. Saltwater Chlorinator Corrosion & Pitting

Saltwater pools splash sodium chloride onto surrounding slabs, penetrating unsealed concrete pores and rusting internal steel rebar. We pour dense N32 strength concrete with 50mm steel cover depth and seal with penetrating silane-siloxane marine sealers.

5. Water Draining BACK Into the Pool

Poor slab fall causes tropical rainwater downpours to flow dirty lawn runoff back into the pool. We establish a 1:40 minimum fall away from the pool shell directed into slimline stainless steel trench drains.

Pool Concreting FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Concrete Pool Surrounds

What is the safest non-slip concrete finish for Gold Coast swimming pools?

Exposed aggregate with smooth rounded quartz pebbles is the #1 non-slip concrete finish for Gold Coast pools. It achieves a P4/P5 slip rating under AS 4586. We add polypropylene grip additives (Shark Grip) directly into silane-siloxane sealers to ensure wet feet never slip around saltwater or chlorine pools.

How do you stop pool surround concrete from getting burning hot in Queensland summer?

To keep pool surrounds cool underfoot during 35°C SEQ summer days, choose light-colored stone aggregate mixes (white quartz, cream, and coastal sandstone) or light-grey/beige spray-on covercrete overlays. Dark charcoal concrete absorbs solar heat, whereas light-reflective oxides keep surface temperatures up to 10°C cooler.

Why is an expansion joint required between the pool shell and surrounding concrete slab?

A fibreglass or concrete pool shell vibrates and moves independently from the surrounding concrete deck during soil moisture changes. A 10mm Ableflex expansion joint sealed with Sikaflex polyurethane must be placed around the pool coping beam. Without this isolation joint, the expansion of the surround slab will shear off pool coping tiles.

How do you prevent pool surround water from draining back into the swimming pool?

We pour pool surround slabs with a strict 1:40 minimum fall (25mm drop per 1 metre of width) directing surface water away from the pool edge into surrounding garden beds or linear stainless steel trench drains.
