Pool Concreting FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Concrete Pool Surrounds

What is the safest non-slip concrete finish for Gold Coast swimming pools?

Exposed aggregate with smooth rounded quartz pebbles is the #1 non-slip concrete finish for Gold Coast pools. It achieves a P4 slip rating under AS 4586. We add polypropylene grip additives (Shark Grip) directly into the sealer to ensure wet feet never slip around salt or chlorine pools.

How do you stop pool surround concrete from getting burning hot in Queensland summer?

To keep pool surrounds cool underfoot during 35°C SEQ summer days, choose light-colored stone aggregate mixes (such as white quartz, cream, and coastal sandstone) or light-grey/beige spray-on covercrete overlays. Dark charcoal concrete absorbs solar heat, whereas light-reflective oxides keep surface temperatures up to 10°C cooler.

Why is an expansion joint required between the pool shell and surrounding concrete slab?

A fibreglass or concrete pool shell vibrates and moves independently from the surrounding concrete deck during soil moisture changes. A 10mm Abelflex expansion joint sealed with Sikaflex polyurethane must be placed around the pool coping beam. Without this isolation joint, the expansion of the surround slab will crack the pool shell coping tile band.
