⚡ Coloured Concrete on the Gold Coast: Key Facts

  • Integral Colour vs Surface Shake: We exclusively pour full-depth integral coloured concrete. Synthetic iron oxide pigments are blended directly into the batch truck, ensuring the color runs through the entire 100mm slab thickness so surface chips never reveal plain grey beneath.
  • Popular Gold Coast Palettes: Deep Charcoal (matches Colorbond Monument), Bluestone Slate, French Vanilla, and Sandstone Beige (harmonizes with coastal dune landscapes).
  • 2026 Average Costs: Coloured concrete typically costs $85 to $120 per m² for supply and install, making it significantly more affordable than exposed aggregate while offering a sleek, contemporary aesthetic.
  • UV Protection & Sealing: High UV levels in South East Queensland require two coats of high-solids solvent acrylic sealer with UV blockers applied after curing to lock in pigment vibrancy and prevent sun fading.

Trade Estimator Note — Gold Coast Concreting Network (QBCC Licensed Partners)

"When pouring coloured concrete, shade consistency is everything. Adding water to the mixer chute on site will dilute the cement paste and turn a dark charcoal into a blotchy light grey. We batch pure N25 or N32 concrete with precise digital oxide dosage from Boral or Concrete Colour Systems (CCS), and finish with a light uniform broom texture for seamless elegance."
