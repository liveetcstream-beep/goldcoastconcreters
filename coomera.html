Quality Standards

Coomera Engineering & Pour Specifications

N25 & N32 Concrete Strength

We use N25 (25MPa) grade concrete for standard paths and shed slabs. For vehicle driveways and heavy traffic, we pour N32 (32MPa) concrete to ensure high load capacity and minimal wear.

Premium Steel Reinforcement

All of our slabs feature standard SL72 steel mesh laid on plastic bar chairs to ensure maximum structural reinforcement in the center of the concrete pour.

Proper Subgrade Compaction

We prevent shifting and cracking by compacting the subgrade and laying a 50mm compacted roadbase layer before installing steel mesh and pouring.

Expansion Control Joints

We cut control joints at precise intervals to prevent cracking and place flexible expansion joints where the new concrete meets the existing house slab or walls.

Professional Execution

Our Concreting Process: Excavation to Sealing

01

Excavation & Base Prep

We excavate to the required depth, remove grass and roots, compact the subgrade using a mechanical plate compactor, and lay a compacted roadbase or crusher dust foundation.

02

Formwork & Steel Laying

We set up straight timber formwork to define the slab edges, lay plastic moisture underlay if required, and position SL72 reinforcement mesh on plastic support chairs.

03

Concrete Pouring & Finishing

We pour concrete (using line pumps if required), level with a screed bar, and apply the final finish—whether it's heavy broom (for crossovers), exposed aggregate wash, or stenciling.

04

Cutting & Sealing

We cut concrete control joints to manage expansion stress and apply two coats of UV-stable solvent acrylic concrete sealer to protect the slab from dirt and stains.

Ground conditions vary significantly across the suburb — from coastal alluvial clay near the Coomera River to engineered fill soil in newly developed housing developments. Ground & Soil Conditions in Coomera

Lower Coomera and waterfront estates rest on alluvial soil pockets influenced by the Coomera River system, whereas Upper Coomera features undulating clay terrain and engineered fill. Unreinforced slabs on fill soil risk uneven settlement and cracking. We mitigate ground movement by excavating to virgin subgrade, laying 50mm compacted roadbase, and placing SL82 heavy steel reinforcement mesh on bar chairs in compliance with AS 2870 (Residential Slabs & Footings).

Frequently Asked Questions - Coomera Concreting

Q: How much does a driveway cost in Coomera?

A: Concreting costs depend on several factors — driveway size, chosen finish (plain, exposed aggregate, or covercrete resurfacing), site access, and excavation requirements. Every property is different, so we don't provide fixed online pricing. Call us on 0411 914 157 or fill out our quick quote form above for a free, accurate, no-obligation quote based on your specific site.

Q: Do I need Gold Coast Council approval for a driveway crossover in Coomera?

A: Yes. Vehicle crossovers constructed across council road reserves in Coomera require a Vehicular Crossing (VXO) permit from the City of Gold Coast. We handle all site drawings, council submissions, and pre-pour compliance inspections.

Q: How do you prevent concrete cracking on fill soil in new Coomera estates?

A: New Coomera developments often sit on fill soil. We prevent cracking by excavating to stable subgrade, compacting a 50mm roadbase sub-base, placing SL82 steel mesh on plastic chairs, and cutting expansion control joints within 24 hours of pouring.

Q: What suburbs near Coomera do you service?

A: We service Upper Coomera, Coomera Waters, Pimpama (4209), Ormeau (4208), Oxenford (4210), Hope Island (4212), and Helensvale (4212).

Q: How long before I can drive on my new driveway?

A: We recommend keeping passenger cars off the concrete for at least 7 days after the pour. Heavy vehicles, caravans, or trailers should be kept off for 28 days to prevent cracks while the concrete cures.
