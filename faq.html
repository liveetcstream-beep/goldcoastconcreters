⚡ Essential Gold Coast Concreting Rules at a Glance

  • Average 2026 Concreting Costs: Plain broom concrete ranges from $75–$100/m²; exposed aggregate ranges from $100–$160/m²; Covercrete resurfacing ranges from $45–$75/m²; and engineered house/shed foundation slabs range from $120–$180/m² depending on site access and excavation.
  • Council Crossover (VXO) Permits: Concreting on public road verges between your property line and the street kerb legally requires a Vehicular Crossing permit from the City of Gold Coast with Form 16 certified trade signoff.
  • Subgrade Engineering (AS 2870): Reactive clay soils (Pimpama, Ormeau, Coomera) and shifting dune sands (Surfers Paradise, Palm Beach, Coolangatta) require a minimum 75mm–100mm compacted roadbase sub-base and SL72/SL82 mesh on plastic bar chairs to eliminate settlement cracks.
  • Vehicle Driving Times: Foot traffic is permitted after 24–48 hours; standard passenger vehicles can drive on new concrete after 7 days; heavy trucks, trade trailers, and campervans should wait a full 28 days for 100% compressive MPa strength cure.

Q: What factors increase the cost of a concreting project?

A: The primary cost variables include: (1) Site excavation & spoil disposal: rock excavation, clay removal, or steep hillside terracing; (2) Concrete pump hire: required if agi mixer trucks cannot reverse directly to the pour site; (3) Slab thickness & steel mesh grade: upgrading from 100mm SL72 mesh to 150mm SL82 mesh for heavy vehicles; (4) Council crossover permit fees: public verge inspections and engineering drawings.

Category 02

Finishes, Colours & Aesthetics

Q: What is the difference between exposed aggregate, plain, and Covercrete?

A: Exposed aggregate is a newly poured structural concrete mix containing natural river stones whose top cement paste is washed off to reveal a slip-resistant pebble texture. Plain concrete is screeded and broom-finished for clean utility. Covercrete is a spray-applied polymer modified cement overlay applied over an existing sound concrete slab to modernize faded or discoloured driveways without demolition.

Q: What are the most popular exposed aggregate colours in Queensland?

A: The top-selling blends across South East Queensland are Salt & Pepper (white, grey, and black river stones), Charcoal Basalt (dark modern charcoal matrix), and Ocean Floor / Golden Amber (warm coastal beach pebble blends from River Sands and Boral).

Q: Is exposed aggregate concrete slippery around swimming pools?

A: No. Exposed aggregate is naturally slip-resistant because the washed stone pebbles provide high tactile grip under wet bare feet. We also incorporate a specialized non-slip micronised silica additive into the final UV-stable acrylic sealer coats for maximum poolside safety.

Category 03

Structural Engineering & Standards (AS 2870 & AS 3600)

Q: What Australian Standards govern residential concreting?

A: Residential concreting on the Gold Coast is governed by:
• AS 2870-2011: Residential Slabs and Footings (soil reactivity, slab thickness, and edge beam sizing).
• AS 3727.1-2016: Pavements - Residential (driveway thicknesses, reinforcement mesh, and control joint spacing).
• AS 3600-2018: Concrete Structures (concrete durability, cover over steel, and marine exposure protection).

Q: How thick should a residential concrete driveway or shed slab be?

A: Under AS 3727.1, standard passenger vehicle driveways require a minimum 100mm slab thickness with SL72 high-tensile mesh over 75mm compacted roadbase. For heavy trade trailers, boats, horse floats, or commercial sheds, we engineer a 125mm–150mm slab with SL82 steel mesh on 65mm chairs.

Q: What concrete MPa strength should be used near the beach?

A: For coastal suburbs within 1km of the ocean (Surfers Paradise, Burleigh Heads, Palm Beach, Coolangatta), we use high-density N32 (32 MPa) concrete with a low water-cement ratio to prevent airborne chloride salt ingress from causing concrete cancer in internal steel reinforcement.

Category 04

City of Gold Coast Council Crossover Permits (VXO)

Q: Do I need a council permit to pour a concrete driveway on the Gold Coast?

A: Driveways poured strictly inside your private property boundary do not require council permits. However, the vehicular crossover—the section connecting your boundary line to the public street kerb—requires a Vehicular Crossing (VXO) permit from the City of Gold Coast before concrete can be poured.

Q: Who manages the council crossover permit application and inspections?

A: Our licensed contractor partners manage the entire crossover process: drafting site drawings, lodging the application with the City of Gold Coast, organizing pre-pour formwork inspections with council officers, and issuing Form 16 certified trade signoffs upon completion.

Category 05

Curing Times, Maintenance & Crack Prevention

Q: How long before I can walk and drive on new concrete?

A: • Foot Traffic: 24 to 48 hours after pouring.
• Passenger Cars & SUVs: 7 days (at which point concrete achieves ~70% design strength).
• Heavy Trucks, Caravans & Boats: Full 28 days to reach 100% design compressive MPa strength.

Q: How do you prevent concrete from cracking?

A: Cracking is eliminated through four mandatory trade steps: (1) Subgrade compaction: 75mm–100mm DGB20 crushed roadbase foundation; (2) Elevated steel mesh: positioning SL72/SL82 mesh on 50mm plastic bar chairs; (3) Diamond saw-cut control joints: cut within 12–24 hours every 3 to 4 metres; (4) Proper curing: preventing rapid moisture loss with curing compounds or aliphatic alcohol during hot Queensland weather.

Q: How often should an exposed aggregate driveway be resealed?

A: In South East Queensland's high UV climate, exposed aggregate and Covercrete surfaces should be pressure cleaned and resealed with a premium solvent-based acrylic sealer every 2 to 3 years to maintain rich stone lustre and protect against motor oil and tannin stains.
