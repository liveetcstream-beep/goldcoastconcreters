Technical Guide

What is Covercrete & How Does Driveway Resurfacing Work?

Covercrete (also referred to as polymer-modified concrete overlay) is an engineered blend of cement, silica sands, synthetic polymers, and UV-stable color pigments spray-applied at 2mm–3mm thickness over structurally sound concrete. It bonds mechanically and chemically to the base concrete, giving you a custom textured slate, tile, or granite finish at a fraction of replacement cost.

Covercrete Resurfacing vs. Full Replacement

Jackhammering out an existing 50m² concrete slab creates heavy noise, disposal waste, and property disruption lasting 5+ days. Covercrete resurfacing achieves the same visual impact in just 1–2 days without damaging surrounding turf or garden beds.

Covercrete vs. Concrete Paint

Hardware store concrete paint coats the surface like plastic film and peels off under hot car tires within months. Covercrete is a mineral-polymer cement compound that becomes part of the slab, engineered for vehicle traffic.

Gold Coast Climate Factors

Why Gold Coast Heat & Moisture Require Specialized Resurfacing

Queensland's subtropical sun creates extreme slab surface temperatures exceeding 60°C in summer. Cheap water-based sealers breakdown under this thermal stress, leading to chalking and flaking. We protect Gold Coast resurfacing jobs by applying two coats of commercial-grade UV solvent acrylic sealer that lock in color pigments and resist hot tire pickup.

Resurfacing Methodology

Our 4-Step Covercrete Spray Process

01

Diamond Grinding & Degreasing

We use heavy industrial walk-behind diamond grinders to remove old paint, oil stains, and smooth glaze, opening the concrete pores to a CSP-2 mechanical bond profile.

02

Crack Repair & Polymer Base Coat

We route out surface cracks, inject high-tensile polymer resin fillers, apply a high-tack acrylic polymer primer, and trowel a smooth polymer base coat.

03

Pattern Taping & Covercrete Spray

We lay custom paper stencils or border tape, then spray two coats of polymer Covercrete compound using specialized hopper gun equipment for an even non-slip texture.

04

Joint Recutting & UV Acrylic Sealing

We pull border tape, recut all expansion joints to prevent stress bridging, and spray two coats of UV-stable solvent acrylic clear sealer to lock in color and shine.

Workmanship Warning

The #1 Reason Cheap Resurfacing Jobs Peel (And How We Fix It)

Over 80% of resurfacing failures are caused by lazy surface preparation. Here is what cheap operators skip:

1. Skipping Industrial Diamond Grinding

Pressure washing alone leaves invisible oil films and smooth glaze. Without mechanical diamond grinding, the new coating cannot bond and will peel off in sheets under vehicle tires. We diamond-grind 100% of every slab surface to CSP-2 profile.

2. Spraying Over Un-repaired Cracks

Simply spraying Covercrete over raw cracks guarantees the cracks will mirror straight through the new coating within weeks. We route out cracks, inject structural polymer resin fillers, and grind smooth before priming.

3. Bridging Expansion Control Joints

Spraying over expansion joints creates rigid bridges that snap as the slab expands under summer heat. We recut and clear all control joints immediately after spraying.

Frequently Asked Questions — Covercrete Resurfacing

Q: How much does Covercrete driveway resurfacing cost on the Gold Coast?

A: Resurfacing costs depend on total square metres, surface preparation required (extent of diamond grinding & crack repair), chosen stencil pattern, and sealer selection. Resurfacing typically costs 50% less than full slab removal. Call 0411 914 157 for a free site measure.

Q: Can severely cracked concrete be resurfaced with Covercrete?

A: Hairline and structural surface cracks can be routed out, resin-injected, and successfully resurfaced. However, if a slab is structurally unlevel or collapsing due to soil washout, full slab replacement may be required.

Q: Will car tires peel or lift the new Covercrete coating?

A: Not when poured by our licensed team! Peeling is caused by skipping mechanical diamond grinding or using cheap water-based paint. We diamond-grind to CSP-2 profile and seal with commercial UV solvent acrylic sealer that resists hot tire pickup.

Q: How long does a Covercrete driveway coating last?

A: With proper initial diamond grinding and regular re-sealing every 3 to 4 years, a professionally applied Covercrete resurfacing finish lasts 10 to 15+ years.

Q: Is Covercrete slip-resistant for wet outdoor patio areas?

A: Yes. We mix anti-slip silica sand grit into the final sealer coat to achieve an R10/R11 slip rating, making it safe for pool surrounds and steep driveways.
