Quality Standards

Pimpama Engineering & Pour Specifications

N25 & N32 Concrete Strength

We pour N25 (25MPa) grade concrete for garden paths and shed slabs. For vehicle driveways and heavy traffic in Pimpama, we use N32 (32MPa) concrete to ensure high load capacity and long-term durability.

SL82 Steel Mesh Reinforcement

Pimpama features reactive black clay soils. All of our slabs feature heavy SL82 steel mesh laid on plastic bar chairs to ensure maximum structural reinforcement in the center of the pour.

Proper Subgrade Compaction

We prevent shifting and cracking by excavating to depth, compacting the subgrade, and laying a 50mm compacted roadbase foundation before installing steel mesh and pouring concrete.

Expansion & Control Joints

We cut control joints at precise intervals to prevent cracking from QLD heat expansion and place flexible expansion joints where new concrete meets house slabs or walls.

Professional Execution

Our Concreting Process: Excavation to Sealing

01

Excavation & Base Prep

We excavate to required depth, remove roots and organic soil, compact subgrade with a plate compactor, and lay a compacted roadbase or crusher dust foundation.

02

Formwork & Steel Laying

We set up straight timber formwork to define slab edges, lay plastic moisture underlay if required, and position SL82 reinforcement mesh on plastic support chairs.

03

Concrete Pouring & Finishing

We pour N32/N25 concrete mix, level with a screed bar, and apply the final finish—whether it's heavy broom (for crossovers), exposed aggregate wash, or stenciling.

04

Cutting & Sealing

We cut concrete control joints to manage expansion stress and apply two coats of UV-stable solvent acrylic concrete sealer to protect the slab from dirt and stains.

Reactive Clay & Soil Dynamics in Pimpama

Pimpama features Class H1 and H2 reactive clay soils that experience extreme volume changes — shrinking during hot QLD droughts and swelling heavily during summer downpours. Standard 100mm unreinforced concrete will crack under this soil movement. We protect Pimpama driveways by excavating to stable subgrade, compacting a 50mm roadbase sub-base, deepening edge beams, and using SL82 heavy steel mesh suspended on plastic bar chairs per AS 2870 (Residential Slabs & Footings).

Q: Why does Pimpama's clay soil require extra concrete reinforcement?

A: Pimpama contains highly reactive clay soil that swells when wet and shrinks when dry. Without proper 50mm roadbase compaction, SL82 steel mesh reinforcement, and control joints, the soil movement will crack plain concrete slabs.

Q: Do I need Gold Coast Council approval for a driveway crossover in Pimpama?
