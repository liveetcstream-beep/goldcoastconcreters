Handling Pimpama's Reactive Clay Soil

Pimpama features heavy black reactive clay soil that expands dramatically during summer downpours and shrinks during dry spells. Without proper sub-grade compaction, 100mm-125mm slab thickness, and heavy SL82 steel mesh reinforcement, concrete slabs will crack. We engineer every pour with proper base preparation to withstand soil movement.

GCCC Crossover (VXO) Permits

Installing a new driveway crossover in Pimpama requires compliance with Gold Coast City Council (GCCC) Vehicular Crossover guidelines. We pour compliant 125mm thick N32 concrete crossovers with SL82 mesh, ensuring you avoid council inspection delays and fines.

UV & Stormwater Protection

Queensland’s intense UV rays and sudden heavy rain events cause unsealed concrete to fade and erode. We apply commercial-grade anti-fade acrylic sealers to all exposed aggregate and coloured concrete driveways to lock in color and prevent oil staining.

Pimpama Concreting FAQs

Do I need a Gold Coast City Council permit for a driveway in Pimpama?

You only need a GCCC Vehicular Crossover (VXO) permit for the section of your driveway extending from your property boundary to the kerb line over council land. Regular driveways within your property boundary do not require council permits.

What concrete mix strength do you use for Pimpama driveways?

We use minimum N25 concrete mix for residential slabs and upgraded N32 high-strength concrete mix for all driveways and heavy crossover areas to guarantee maximum durability.

How long before I can park my car on a new concrete driveway in Pimpama?

Foot traffic is safe after 24–48 hours. However, we recommend waiting a full 7 days before driving or parking passenger vehicles on your new concrete slab to allow proper curing.

What steel mesh reinforcement is best for Pimpama's clay soil?

We install SL82 steel reinforcement mesh elevated on plastic chairs to ensure the steel sits dead in the middle of the concrete pour, giving maximum tensile strength against clay movement.

Are your concreting contractors QBCC licensed?

Yes. All work is carried out by fully qualified, QBCC licensed, and insured concreters in compliance with Australian Standard AS 3600.
