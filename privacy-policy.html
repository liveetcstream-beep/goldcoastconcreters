Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 10, 2026 | Compliant with Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth)

1. Introduction

At Gold Coast Concreters QLD (accessible at goldcoastconcretersqld.com.au), protecting the privacy and confidentiality of our website visitors and quote requesters is paramount. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, store, and disclose your personal information in compliance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information when you fill out our online quote forms, request an on-site estimate, or communicate with us via phone or electronic channels. This information may include:

  • Your full name and contact telephone number.
  • Your property address, suburb, and postcode on the Gold Coast.
  • Project details (e.g. driveway dimensions, slab requirements, requested finishes like exposed aggregate or covercrete).
  • Technical website usage data (e.g. IP address, browser type, referring pages, and cookies captured via Google Analytics).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the personal information collected for the primary purpose of delivering concreting estimation, site measure, and trade contracting services. Specifically, your data is used to:

  • Contact you regarding your requested concreting quote or site inspection.
  • Refer your quote request details to QBCC-licensed concrete trade partners operating in your local Gold Coast suburb.
  • Analyze website performance and optimize local user experience.
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations in Queensland, Australia.

4. Referral to QBCC Licensed Contractors

Gold Coast Concreters QLD operates as a digital lead generation and marketing portal. To fulfill your request for physical concreting estimates, site measures, and slab construction, your contact and project details may be shared with qualified, licensed trade partners holding active Queensland Building and Construction Commission (QBCC) licenses.

5. Cookies and Web Analytics

Our website utilizes cookies and third-party tracking scripts (such as Google Tag Manager G-BJDBPFFRCW) to monitor website traffic and user interaction. Cookies are small text files stored on your browser. You may adjust your browser settings to decline cookies; however, some site functionalities may be altered.

6. Data Security and Retention

We implement industry-standard security measures (including SSL encryption and secure form transmission via Web3Forms) to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill trade quote processing and legal requirements.

7. Access and Correction

Under the Australian Privacy Principles, you have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, or to request corrections if any details are inaccurate. To make an access or correction request, please contact our privacy officer at support@goldcoastconcretersqld.com.au.
