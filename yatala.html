Yatala Engineering & Heavy-Duty Specs

N32 & N40 Concrete Mix Strength

We pour high-capacity N32 (32MPa) and heavy industrial N40 (40MPa) concrete mixes engineered specifically for truck loadings, forklift traffic, and industrial slabs in Yatala.

SL82 / SL92 Double Mesh Reinforcement

Industrial slabs feature heavy SL82 or SL92 steel mesh suspended on heavy-duty plastic bar chairs to guarantee maximum structural tensile strength and prevent sagging.

125mm - 150mm Sub-base Compaction

We excavate to depth, lay a 100mm-150mm compacted roadbase foundation, and mechanically compact the subgrade to eliminate sub-surface movement under heavy axle weights.

Heavy Vehicle Crossover Approvals

All Yatala commercial crossover installations comply strictly with Gold Coast City Council (GCCC) heavy vehicle crossing standards (VXO) and engineering drawings.

Professional Execution

Our Concreting Process: Excavation to Sealing

01

Excavation & Site Preparation

We excavate to industrial depth, clear spoil, compact the subgrade with heavy rollers, and install a compacted roadbase or crusher dust sub-base.

02

Formwork & Steel Placement

We set up rigid steel/timber formwork, lay heavy plastic moisture barriers, and position SL82/SL92 reinforcement mesh on plastic support chairs.

03

Concrete Pouring & Power Floating

We pour N32/N40 concrete mix via concrete pumps, power screed to level, and trowel/finish—whether helicopter power float, broom finish, or aggregate wash.

04

Stress Saw Cutting & Sealing

We saw-cut contraction joints to control thermal cracking and apply two coats of heavy-duty commercial acrylic or epoxy concrete sealer.
