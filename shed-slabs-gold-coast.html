Concrete Shed Slabs Gold Coast: Engineered Bases for Workshops, Garages & Machinery Sheds

Whether you are installing a residential garden shed in Pimpama (4209), a heavy-duty workshop in Coomera (4209), or a commercial machinery shed in Yatala (4207), a rock-solid, level concrete slab is essential. Our licensed concreters pour custom-engineered shed foundations built strictly to AS 2870 (Residential Slabs) and AS 3600 (Concrete Structures), featuring integrated perimeter footings, weather rebate edges, and heavy steel reinforcement.

⚡ Shed Slab Specifications Quick Guide

  • Garden Shed Slabs (up to 3m x 3m): 100mm slab thickness, N25 concrete mix, SL62 mesh, 50mm roadbase subgrade.
  • Workshop & Double Garage Slabs: 100mm–125mm slab, N25/N32 concrete, SL72 mesh on chairs, 300mm x 300mm perimeter beam footings.
  • Industrial & Heavy Machinery Sheds: 150mm+ slab thickness, N32 MPa concrete, SL82 dual mesh layers, engineered hold-down rag bolt cages.
  • Waterproofing & Rebate: 0.2mm high-impact polythene vapor barrier + 50mm x 35mm weather rebate edge detail.

1. Soil Soil Classification & Perimeter Footing Design

Gold Coast hinterland and coastal soils present severe challenges for shed footings:

  • Northern Clay Belt (Ormeau 4208, Kingsholme 4208, Willow Vale 4209): Highly reactive soils (Class H1 and H2) expand during heavy summer rainstorms and contract sharply in dry periods. Without deep perimeter beam footings (typically 300mm wide by 400mm deep reinforced with N12 trench mesh), a shed slab can tilt or snap at the corners under wind uplift.
  • Acreage & Sloped Blocks (Oxenford 4210, Helensvale 4212, Kingsholme 4208): Require cut-and-fill site preparation, heavy subgrade compaction, and retaining wall integration before placing forms.

3. Critical Detailing: Weather Rebates & Hold-Down Bolts

A. Watertight Weather Rebate Edge

A weather rebate is a recessed 50mm wide by 30mm–40mm deep step formed into the perimeter of the wet slab. When your steel shed wall cladding is fixed, the lower metal sheets extend down past the interior floor line into this rebate. This prevents rainwater from seeping under the wall sheets during Gold Coast summer thunderstorms, keeping your tools and equipment bone dry.

B. Hold-Down Bolt Placement (Uplift Resistance)

Gold Coast experiences high wind velocity zones (Region B classification). Shed portal frames require massive uplift resistance. We precisely set cast-in J-bolts or chemical ChemSet studs into the wet concrete footings according to your shed manufacturer's engineering drawings (such as Fairplay Sheds, Ranbuild, or Aussie Sheds specifications).

Trade Quality Guarantee: We lay a continuous 0.2mm high-impact polythene vapor barrier (Visqueen) under 100% of the slab area, overlapping seams by 200mm and taping all joints. This prevents rising damp from destroying timber workbenches or rusting stored metal tools.

4. Our 5-Step Shed Slab Pouring Process

  1. Site Excavation & Subgrade Compaction: Clear vegetation, excavate edge footings, lay 75mm roadbase, and compact with heavy plate compactors.
  2. Formwork & Rebate Setting: Erect rigid timber formwork set precisely to laser level, including the perimeter rebate boards.
  3. Vapor Barrier & Steel Placement: Install 0.2mm plastic membrane, bar chairs, SL72 steel mesh, and trench mesh in footings.
  4. Concrete Placement & Screeding: Pour N25/N32 concrete sourced from local batch plants (Boral/Hanson), bull-float level, and apply smooth steel trowel or non-slip broom finish.
  5. Form Removal & QBCC Certification: Strip formwork, inspect perimeter, and issue Form 16 inspection documentation.

5. Frequently Asked Questions

How thick should a concrete shed slab be on the Gold Coast?

For light lawnmower or garden sheds up to 3m x 3m, a 100mm slab thickness with SL62 mesh is standard. For residential workshops, double garages, or heavy machinery sheds, engineering under AS 2870 requires a 100mm to 150mm slab thickness reinforced with SL72 mesh and 300mm x 300mm perimeter beam footings.

What is a weather rebate edge and why is it mandatory for steel sheds?

A weather rebate (typically 50mm wide by 30mm–40mm deep) is a recessed ledge formed around the slab perimeter. The wall cladding sheets of your steel shed extend down into this rebate below the floor line, creating a watertight barrier that prevents wind-driven storm water from flooding the shed interior.

Do I need a QBCC Form 16 Inspection Certificate for my shed slab?

Yes. If your shed requires Council approval or building certifier sign-off, a QBCC Form 16 (Aspect Inspection Certificate) is mandatory. As licensed contractors (QBCC #1524819), we sign off on subgrade compaction, moisture barrier placement, and steel mesh reinforcement inspection.

Should shed hold-down bolts be cast in during the pour or dynabolted later?

Cast-in rag bolts or J-bolts set into wet concrete during the pour provide significantly higher uplift resistance against Gold Coast severe storm winds. However, if your shed manufacturer specifies sleeve anchors or ChemSet chemical anchors, we install heavy edge beam footings to support post-pour drilling.

How long after pouring a shed slab can the steel shed be erected?

Concrete reaches over 70% of its structural design strength after 7 days. You can begin assembling wall portal frames and standing roof trusses after 5 to 7 days, but full vehicle or heavy machinery loads should be delayed until the full 28-day cure.
