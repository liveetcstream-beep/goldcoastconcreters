Our Concreting Process: Excavation to Sealing

01

Acreage Excavation & Drainage Grading

We cut and grade the slope, remove organic topsoil, install drainage channels, and roll 100mm of crushed DGB20 roadbase sub-base.

02

Formwork, Edge Beams & SL82 Mesh

We build heavy-duty formwork, excavate thickened perimeter footings for shed slabs, and tie SL82 steel mesh securely on plastic support chairs.

03

Boom Pumping & High-Traction Finish

We pump certified N25/N32 concrete into position across difficult terrain, screed to precise falls, and apply heavy broom or exposed pebble finishes.

04

Saw-Cutting & Penetrating Sealer

We cut thermal stress relief joints within 24 hours and apply two coats of premium UV-stable solvent acrylic sealer for maximum durability.

Mudgeeraba FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions — Mudgeeraba Concreting

Q: How much does an acreage concrete driveway cost in Mudgeeraba (4213)?

A: Acreage driveways in Mudgeeraba typically range from $85 to $130 per square metre for broom finish and $100 to $150 per square metre for exposed aggregate, depending on length, slope steepness, excavation, and concrete pump access. Call 0411 914 157 for a free site measure.

Q: How thick should a machinery or workshop shed slab be in Mudgeeraba?

A: For light garden sheds, a 100mm slab with SL72 mesh is sufficient. For heavy horse floats, tractors, or trade workshops, we pour a 125mm–150mm slab with SL82 steel mesh and 300mm reinforced perimeter edge footings per AS 2870.

Q: Can you pour concrete driveways on steep Mudgeeraba hills?

A: Yes! We specialize in steep hillside concreting using concrete line and boom pumps, reinforced edge formwork, step downs, and heavy broom or exposed aggregate traction finishes that allow safe vehicle ascent in all weather.

Q: How do you handle stormwater drainage on sloping acreage blocks?

A: We engineer cross-fall grading, install concrete spoon drains, and connect heavy-duty surface grates to redirect heavy hinterland rainwater runoff away from house foundations and down slopes safely.

Q: Do I need a Council permit for a rural driveway crossover in Mudgeeraba?

A: Yes. All driveway crossings extending from your property boundary over the road verge or drainage swale require a Vehicular Crossing (VXO) permit from the City of Gold Coast. We handle site drawings, pipe culvert installs, and Form 16 signoffs for you.
